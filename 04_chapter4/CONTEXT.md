# Chapter 4 Context — The Dawn of Post-Scarcity and the Transformation of Value

## Scope
This chapter envisions the post-scarcity society enabled by converging technologies, exploring how value, motivation, and social organization are transformed when material needs are universally met.

## Key Themes
- Post-scarcity: when material needs are universally met at negligible cost
- Money replaced by reputation, contribution, and social capital
- AI-managed resource allocation and blockchain transparency
- The experiential and creative economies
- Motivation and meaning without economic necessity
- New political divides: techno-socialism vs. corporate libertarianism
- Humanist vs. post-humanist visions
- Space colonization and off-world expansion
