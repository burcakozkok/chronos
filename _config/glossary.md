# Glossary — Chronos

## Economic and Social Terms

**Capitalism**
An economic system characterized by private ownership of the means of production, wage labor, and the pursuit of profit through market exchange.

**Post-Capitalism**
Any economic system that succeeds capitalism, typically characterized by collective or democratic ownership of productive assets, reduced role of wage labor, and universal provision of basic needs.

**Post-Scarcity**
A hypothetical condition in which the production of goods and services is so advanced and efficient that material needs are universally met at negligible cost.

**Universal Basic Income (UBI)**
A policy in which all citizens receive a regular, unconditional cash payment from the government, regardless of employment status.

**Deflation**
A sustained decrease in the general price level of goods and services. In post-capitalist context, may be benign (driven by technological abundance) rather than malign (driven by collapsing demand).

**DAO (Distributed Autonomous Organization)**
A blockchain-based organization governed by smart contracts and collective voting, without centralized management.

**Smart Contract**
A self-executing contract with the terms of the agreement directly written into code on a blockchain.

**Cryptocurrency**
A digital or virtual currency secured by cryptography, operating on decentralized blockchain networks.

**Capital Income**
Income derived from ownership of assets — rent, dividends, interest — as opposed to income from labor.

**Wage Income**
Income earned through employment — the exchange of labor for money.

**Serfdom**
A feudal system in which agricultural laborers (serfs) are bound to the land and obligated to work for the landowner, but are not owned as property (unlike slaves).

## Technological Terms

**AI (Artificial Intelligence)**
Computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence — learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, language understanding.

**Automation**
The use of technology to perform tasks with minimal human intervention.

**Robotics**
The design, construction, and operation of robots — machines capable of carrying out complex physical tasks.

**Carbon Capture**
Technologies that remove carbon dioxide from the atmosphere or at the point of emission, to mitigate climate change.

**Asteroid Mining**
The extraction of minerals and resources from asteroids in space, proposed as a solution to terrestrial resource depletion.

**Blockchain**
A distributed ledger technology in which records are stored in linked blocks across a network of computers, making them tamper-resistant.

## Historical and Philosophical Terms

**Technological Determinism**
The theory that technological innovation is the primary driver of social and historical change.

**Historical Materialism**
Marx’s theory that the material conditions of production — technology, resources, labor — are the foundation of social organization and historical change.

**Axial Age**
A term coined by Karl Jaspers for the period ~800–200 BCE, during which major philosophical and spiritual traditions emerged independently across Greece, India, China, Persia, and Israel.

**Bronze Age Collapse**
A period (~1200–1150 BCE) of widespread societal collapse across the Eastern Mediterranean, destroying palace economies and trade networks, and preceding the Axial Age.

**Cognitive Revolution**
The emergence of modern human cognition ~70,000 BCE, enabling language, abstract thought, and complex social cooperation.

**Demographic Transition**
The shift from high birth and death rates (pre-industrial) to low birth and death rates (post-industrial), resulting in population stabilization or decline.
