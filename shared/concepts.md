# Core Concepts — Chronos

## The Production Equation

```
production = land × labor × capital × techlevel
```

Where:
- **land** = natural resources, territory, environment
- **labor** = human workforce (quantity and quality)
- **capital** = money invested in equipment, software, infrastructure
- **techlevel** = the multiplier effect of technology on all other factors

Comparison with Cobb-Douglas:
```
Q = A · L^α · K^β
```
Where A = total factor productivity (technology), L = labor, K = capital, α and β = output elasticities.

Key insight: in a post-capitalist, highly automated economy, **techlevel** dominates the equation. Labor and capital become less determinative as AI and automation substitute for them.

## The Three Sources of Personal Income

1. **Wage Income** — earned through employment; declining in post-capitalist transition as automation displaces labor.
2. **Capital Income** — rent, dividends, interest from asset ownership; may be collectivized or replaced by UBI in post-capitalist models.
3. **Grants** — UBI, welfare, universal services; rising as wages decline.

**Post-capitalist income trajectory:**
- Wages ↓ (declining)
- Capital income ↔ (restructured or collectivized)
- Grants ↑ (rising, possibly dominant)

## Deflation in Post-Capitalism

In a post-capitalist, highly automated economy:
- **Benign deflation:** driven by technological abundance and productivity gains; prices fall, real living standards rise.
- **Malign deflation:** driven by collapsing demand; damaging in capitalist economies but mitigated in post-capitalist models where basic needs are guaranteed.

Asset ownership logic in deflation:
- Holding cash is rational (purchasing power rises).
- Owning assets (shares, businesses) is rational **only if** their growth outpaces deflation — i.e., if technology-driven growth exceeds the deflation rate.

## The Cause-and-Effect Chain (Post-Capitalist Transition)

1. Climate change → resource shortages → green tech innovation
2. Aging population → labor shortage → automation and AI
3. Automation → job displacement → poverty and polarization
4. Poverty → UBI, collectivization, worker cooperatives
5. Government spending → currency debasement → crypto adoption
6. Polarization → authoritarianism, surveillance, border controls
7. AI/robotics → data economy → personal data as income source
8. Resource shortages → space technology → asteroid mining, space colonization
9. DAOs + blockchain → decentralized governance and finance
10. Deflation + tech growth → new asset ownership logic
