# Master Civilizational Timeline — Chronos

## Deep History

| Date | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| ~13.8 billion BCE | Big Bang | Origin of the universe |
| ~4.5 billion BCE | Earth forms | Origin of our world |
| ~3.8 billion BCE | First life | Origin of biology |
| ~300,000 BCE | Homo sapiens emerges | Origin of our species |
| ~70,000 BCE | **Cognitive Revolution** | Language, abstract thought, social cooperation; enables complex civilization |
| ~50,000 BCE | Out of Africa migration | Homo sapiens spreads across the globe |
| ~10,000 BCE | **Agricultural Revolution** | Settled communities, surplus food, social hierarchy, property |
| ~5,000 BCE | **Urban Revolution** | Cities, writing, trade, governance, early states |
| ~3,200 BCE | Writing invented (Sumer) | Record-keeping, law, literature |
| ~1,200–1,150 BCE | **Bronze Age Collapse** | Widespread civilizational collapse; destroys palace economies; precedes Axial Age |
| ~800–200 BCE | **Axial Age (Philosophical Revolution)** | Independent emergence of philosophy, ethics, and spiritual traditions (Greece, India, China, Persia, Israel) |
