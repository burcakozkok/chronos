# Chapter 6 Context — The Paradox of Abundance: Economy, Demography, and the Meaning of Progress

## Scope
This chapter weaves together economic, demographic, and philosophical threads to explore the paradoxes of abundance: how prosperity transforms meaning, purpose, and the very definition of progress.

## Key Themes
- Post-scarcity economy: when abundance erodes old measures of wealth
- Deflation as a sign of progress rather than crisis
- Aging societies: priorities shift from growth to well-being
- The meaning economy: creativity, experience, and purpose as value
- Virtual worlds and the expansion of the possible
- Risks: inequality, existential vacuum, social fragmentation
- Redefining progress: beyond GDP toward well-being
- Policy responses: UBI, ethical AI governance, new metrics
