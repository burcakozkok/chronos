# Chronos — Project Context

## Purpose
Chronos is a long-form essay exploring the arc of human civilization — from hunter-gatherer societies to post-capitalist futures — written in the style of Carl Sagan. It weaves together technological determinism, Marxist stage theory, demographic change, environmental pressures, and post-scarcity economics into a coherent, narrative-driven vision of the next 25 years and beyond.

## Core Thesis
Technological innovation is the primary trigger for transitions between civilizational stages. Each stage is defined by its dominant mode of production and class relations:
- Hunter-Gatherer → Agricultural (trigger: agriculture)
- Agricultural/Slavery → Feudalism (trigger: heavy plow, animal harness)
- Feudalism → Capitalism (trigger: industrial machines, steam engine)
- Capitalism → Post-Capitalism (trigger: AI, automation, renewable energy, space technology)

## Income Framework
Three kinds of personal income:
1. **Wage income** — declining in post-capitalist transition
2. **Capital income** — rent, dividends (may be collectivized)
3. **Grants** — UBI, universal services (rising in post-capitalist transition)
