# Chapter 3 Context — The Age of Automation and the Reimagining of Value

## Scope
This chapter explores the rise of AI, automation, and digital networks, and how these technologies are reshaping work, value, and social organization. It introduces the deflationary paradox and the emergence of new economic models.

## Key Themes
- AI, robotics, and automation as civilizational-scale technologies
- The end of wage labor as the primary source of income
- The deflationary paradox: abundance erodes traditional economic measures
- Rise of the service and experience economy
- Universal Basic Income as a response to technological unemployment
- DAOs and collective ownership models
- Data as a new income source for individuals
- The reimagining of money and value

## Revolutions to Weave In
- AI Revolution (2000–present) as the current primary trigger
- Space technology revolution (2025–) as a parallel driver
