# Chronos

> *Named after the Greek god of time — a meditation on the long arc of civilization, from the first hunter-gatherers to the post-capitalist futures that await us among the stars.*

## About

Chronos is an essay project exploring the history and future of human civilization through the lens of technological determinism, social evolution, demographic change, and post-capitalist economics. The narrative is written in the style of Carl Sagan — clear, poetic, and rooted in scientific and humanistic perspective.
