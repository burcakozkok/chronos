# Chapter 5 Context — Beyond Money: The Structure and Struggles of Post-Scarcity Society

## Scope
This chapter examines the structural features of post-scarcity society in detail: new political divides, governance models, technological cascades, demographic shifts, and the spectrum of utopian and dystopian outcomes.

## Key Themes
- Reputation and contribution as currency
- Resource-based allocation and participatory governance
- New political fault lines: identity, autonomy, cosmic purpose
- The technology tree: cascading waves of innovation (2025–2300+)
- Demography and deflation in the meaning economy
- Dystopian risks: power concentration, ennui, authoritarianism
- Utopian possibilities: creativity, equity, cosmic exploration
