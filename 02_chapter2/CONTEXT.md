# Chapter 2 Context — The Pressures of Our Time

## Scope
This chapter explores the environmental and social pressures of the 21st century that are driving technological innovation and social transformation. It establishes the urgency and complexity of the transition we are living through.

## Key Themes
- Climate change: resource shortages, extreme weather, health crises
- Pollution and biodiversity loss
- Social inequality and polarization
- Migration and displacement
- Erosion of democratic norms and civil liberties
- Technological responses: renewables, carbon capture, AI, desalination
- The feedback loop between environmental pressure and innovation

## Revolutions to Weave In
- Digital Revolution (~1969–2000) as precursor to current AI-driven transformation
- The ongoing AI Revolution (2000–present) as the current technological trigger
